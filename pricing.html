Sizing Guide

Match Your Laundry to the Right Drum

Avoid paying for extra capacity you don’t need, or overcrowding smaller machines which slows down cleaning efficiency.

Small Load

Up to 8 kg capacity
  • Household Size: 1–2 individuals
  • Fits Approximately: 20 shirts, 2 pairs of jeans, underwear & gym apparel
  • Recommended Setup: Standard Washer (8 kg) + Express Dryer
