Pro Tips

Before You Start the Dryer.

A few simple habits prevent garment shrinkage, protect elastics, and cut drying time significantly.

01
Check Care Labels Verify tumble dry compatibility before transferring garments into the machine.
02
Separate Fabrics Dry lightweight synthetics separately from thick towels to avoid over-drying delicates.
03
Avoid Overloading Leave sufficient space in the drum so hot air can circulate freely through every layer.
04
Select Proper Heat Use Low heat for synthetics/sportswear and Medium/High for durable 100% cottons.
05
Remove Promptly Take laundry out when the cycle ends to prevent settling wrinkles and ease folding.
