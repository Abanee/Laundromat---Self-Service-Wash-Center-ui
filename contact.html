Frequently Asked Visit Questions

Helpful answers regarding parking, rush hours, attendant support, and immediate terminal assistance.

Weekday mornings between 6:00 AM and 11:00 AM, and afternoons from 2:00 PM to 4:30 PM generally have the lowest foot traffic, with 80%+ of all drums immediately available. Weekends (especially Saturday morning) are our busiest windows.
Yes. Our Downtown Flagship has 8 dedicated client parking spaces directly behind the facility. The Westside Express Hub features adjacent curbside street parking and dual EV charging bays. The University Metro branch is adjacent to public transit parking and offers secure bicycle racks.
If an attendant is on duty, please inform them at the counter for an immediate machine reallocation. If you are washing outside attendant hours, press the red "Help / Refund" button directly on the terminal or call our 24/7 hotline at +91 (0) 800-927-4482 for instant remote credit.
While self-service cycles are the user's primary responsibility, our attendants routinely monitor finished machines to ensure clothes are safe. You can also opt for our "Wash & Transfer" service at the counter for ₹30, where an attendant will transfer your load to a dryer while you are away.
Commercial accounts can be established within 24 hours. Once you submit our corporate inquiry form above or email business@washhub.in, we provide a formal rate quote, assign your pickup window, and issue your corporate account portal login.
