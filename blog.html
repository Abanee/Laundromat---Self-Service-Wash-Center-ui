Wash Center Principles

Small laundry habits make a big difference.

Great laundry isn't about complicated machines. It’s about five simple, deliberate habits that protect fiber texture, preserve vibrant color, and double garment lifespan.

01

Pre-Sort by Weight & Color Before You Arrive

Keep heavy hoodies away from lightweight shirts, and separate dark dyes from crisp whites to eliminate friction pilling and color transfer.

02

Select Temperature Based on Soil, Not Tradition

Modern softened water cleans everyday clothes at 30°C–40°C. Reserve 60°C thermal sanitization for bed linens and bath towels.

03

Respect the Drum Breathing Room

Leave a hand's span between the top of your laundry and the drum crown so clothing has space to agitate and rinse freely.

04

Match Dryer Temperature to Fabric Fiber

Use low heat for activewear and synthetics to avoid fiber melting, and medium-high heat for dense 100% cotton linens.

05

Remove and Shake Out Promptly

Unloading clothes while warm and giving each piece a quick shake releases 90% of set wrinkles, virtually eliminating the need to iron.
